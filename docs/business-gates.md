# Commercial gates — no millionaire promise

The code is only one part of a business. The product must earn repeat use and paid demand before we expand to other traditions.

## First user cohort

Recruit 50–100 people who genuinely use Vedic charts. Measure:

- Successful birth-chart completion rate.
- Calculation disagreements against independently selected reference charts.
- Seven-day return rate and 30-day return rate.
- Which report sections users save, share, or revisit.
- Whether users will actually pay for a detailed report, not just say they would.

Do not add subscriptions until users show a repeat-use reason. First test a one-off paid report; then test recurring value such as a well-explained dasha/transit calendar.

## Illustrative revenue arithmetic

At a hypothetical price of US$9.99/month, 8,342 paying subscribers would be about US$1.0 million in annual recurring revenue before payment fees, taxes, refunds, hosting, support, and other costs. This is not a forecast, profit, valuation, or personal wealth. If 3% of monthly active users converted to paid, that scenario would require roughly 278,000 monthly active users. The 3% is an assumption, not observed conversion data.

## Differentiation we can actually build

1. Verifiable calculation settings and per-planet ephemeris provenance.
2. Clear separation of calculated facts from traditional interpretations.
3. Privacy controls and export/delete support.
4. Useful free chart pages that can be discovered through search, with paid depth only after trust is earned.
5. Native-quality language support, starting with English and Hindi, then Gujarati and other demand-led languages.

## Expansion gate

Do not start Western, Tarot, Chinese, or Numerology apps because the shared architecture makes them easy to scaffold. Add the next product only after Vedic has meaningful repeat use, paid demand, stable calculation quality, and a supportable acquisition channel.
